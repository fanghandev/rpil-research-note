# Phase 0: gammart vs classic formulas

Ratios are gammart / formula, mean +- SD over the modules that see the point (n). g_paix = w_s^2 cos^3 / (4 pi r^2) with the Paix width, g_nom with the nominal w. `det` = deterministic first-interaction probability (parallel pixel scene, summed over pixels); `MC` = gammart MC primary first-interaction probability. Rg(MC) = FWHM of first-interaction positions carried to the mean-depth plane x b / f_eff (continuous, no pixels), vs d_re (b+f)/f (pen.) and w (b+f)/f (nominal). R = FWHM of the in-window primary readout events binned in the real pixels x b / f_eff, vs sqrt(Rg^2 + Ri^2).
